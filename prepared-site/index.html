Frequently Asked Questions

What are commercial and private legal rights?

commercial and private legal rights are legal protections for creations of the mind, including artistic, literary and musical works, designs, inventions, and business assets, like brand names and logos. There are several types of rights, such as corporate law, dispute resolution, and real estate. A rights owner can use their law as they wish, and they’re protected against others using their law without permission.

What does an lawyer do?

lawyers help individuals and businesses properly register, protect, and manage their law assets through various non-contentious (e.g. registering a right) and contentious (e.g. fighting an infringement) work. This is vital for ensuring owners of law maximise the commercial value of their work.

How are your fees structured?

We can provide the majority of our services on a fixed fee basis. Our lawyers will scope out the nature of the work required and provide you with a fixed fee quote to tackle the next steps.

What should I do if someone is infringing my commercial and private legal rights?

If someone is infringing your commercial and private legal rights, you should collect as much evidence as you can. For example, screenshots, website links, and photos. Next, you should speak to a solicitor, who can advise whether you have grounds for a complaint and what steps to take next.
