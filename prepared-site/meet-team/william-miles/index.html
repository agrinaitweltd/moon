Managing Partner

OCHORA EDWARD LABEJA

Ochora Edward Labeja is the Managing Partner of Moonstone Advocates and an Advocate of the High Court of Uganda. He provides partner-led support to businesses, institutions and individuals on corporate, commercial, employment, property, dispute resolution and family matters.

Edward is valued for practical legal judgment, direct client attention and commercially aware advice that helps clients move from uncertainty to clear legal action.

Clients deserve more than legal opinions; they deserve committed legal partners.

Moonstone Advocates provides practical, partner-led legal support from Kampala, Uganda.

Background &
Qualifications

William completed his legal studies with a distinction from the College of Law, Guildford. He joined Moonstone Advocates in 2014 after training and qualifying at a boutique litigation practice where he acted for a wide variety of clients including international hair care brands and highly acclaimed sports personalities.

William is a dual qualified solicitor (England & Wales and Uganda). He is a member of the Law Society of England & Wales, the Law Society of Uganda and the Chartered Institute of Trade Mark Attorneys (CITMA). He has been recognised as a ‘Recommended Lawyer’ in the Legal500.
